About cached imports

Where the data lives. Once you import an Excel file, the asset list is stored in your browser's local cache so you don't have to re-import every time you open this floor.

What survives. Refreshing or reopening the file keeps your cached imports, markers, statuses, and newcomers intact.

What clears it. Clearing browser cookies, site data, or running this in a private/incognito window will remove the cache. Different browsers or devices each have their own cache.

How to be safe. When you're done surveying, open the Data modal and run Backup AVScout. That packs every floor and its survey into a downloadable .zip you can keep — or share with a colleague — independent of any cache.
